About this model
  • A gradient boosting model trained on a public Kaggle dataset of 1,338 US records, using age, sex, BMI and smoking status.
  • On held-out test data it explains about 85% of the variation in charges (R² 0.85).
  • Supported ranges: age 18–64, BMI 18.5–40.
  • It can miss rare, very high bills among non-smokers.
  • The dataset states no time period or currency, so amounts are illustrative. This is an estimate, not an insurance quote.
